02 · How it works

Four steps, one root.

Money moves through the bank as usual. Shaffof only keeps the ledger and makes it impossible to change quietly.

01

Money stays in the bank

Income and spending go through the fund’s own bank account. Shaffof never touches money.

02

Entries are anonymous

Date, amount, category and a document hash. No donor names.

03

Sealed every month

Entries fold into a Merkle tree; the root is timestamped with OpenTimestamps.

04

Donors verify

With a receipt number: is my entry under this root? The answer is a proof, not trust.

    05 · Who it is for

    For those who want to show trust with proof.

    Charity funds

    Publish the report and seal its root. Don’t ask for trust, give proof.

    Mosque and mahalla charity committees

    A small ledger and a simple page: income, spending and a monthly seal.

    Donors

    Find your own entry by receipt number. Your name never appears anywhere.

    06 · Principles

    What we will not do, said first.

    We never touch money

    Shaffof does not collect, hold or move funds. Payments go through the fund’s bank account.

    No token, no crypto payments

    The seal only ties a hash to a time. Nobody buys any crypto-asset.

    Donor privacy

    No names in the public report: only date, amount, category and hash.

    No rankings

    No donor lists, no “most generous” tables, no points. We are discussing the risk of riya (showing off) with scholars.

    No fatwas

    Methods come with their sources. Qualified scholars review them in writing before release.

    Open source

    Apache-2.0. Read the code before trusting anyone, including us.
